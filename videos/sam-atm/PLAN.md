# Sam at the ATM

Sam withdraws a small stack of cash, brings it into a close-up, and slides it into his open wallet.

Settings: 10 seconds, 1920 x 1080, 30 fps, 2D. No narration or music. ATM and paper/wallet effects.
Scope: new standalone film only; existing Remotion compositions and rigs are unchanged. Sam uses the current hairless, white-face, blue-hoodie identity with the stickman skill rig.

One world: floor y=960; Sam x=880, y=548, scale=.8; ATM body x=1045..1425, y=330..960; cash outlet x=1050..1230, y=665; open wallet center x=858, mouth y=686. Cash and both hand targets share the same timeline.
