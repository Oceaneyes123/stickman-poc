# How a War Thousands of Miles Away Reaches Your Wallet — Sources

Historical case anchored in Russia’s full-scale invasion of Ukraine on February 24, 2022. U.S. examples show how the shock reached households far from the fighting. Somalia illustrates a different degree of exposure. The assumed audience is English-speaking adults familiar with filling up, buying groceries and paying bills.

## Editorial assumptions

**A01.** The driver, oil buyers, bakery purchasing arrangements, household choices and debt comparison are illustrations. They carry no personal testimony, invented transaction totals or assumed loan terms. Price figures are historical series values. The title’s distance is from the U.S. viewer’s example.

**A02.** Any pump display, map, invoice, label or contract in the visual notes is an original graphic candidate. Credit redrawn data by source and period, and label illustrative objects where needed. Archive images require an asset-specific authenticity and rights review before production.
