# Episode brief

- **Title:** The Buy Now, Pay Later Trap Nobody Notices
- **Viewer:** A working adult who has used a "pay in four" checkout, or is about to, and has not connected those payments to a tighter month later.
- **Question:** Why does a purchase someone already refused become easy to accept once the price is split?
- **Promise:** Show that dividing a price changes the size of the decision, not the size of the cost, and that the consequence is less room rather than less money.
- **Story engine:** Scenario plus a fair comparison — one person, one button, then a friend who asks a different question.
- **Core concept:** A payment plan relocates the felt cost in time and shrinks the number in front of the decision. Nothing about the goods changes.
- **Target:** 10–12 minutes at natural pace, subject to measured narration. No speed changes to reach the target.
- **Mechanism:** The plan splits one obligation into separate dated schedules that never appear together; the decision is made against the instalment while the whole price still comes due.
- **Sam's motive:** He wants a good jacket, he has already declined it twice at full price, and the offer meets him at an ordinary moment. Treat him with respect; he is not reckless.
- **Alex's tradeoff:** He asks one question at the counter — would I buy this as one payment? — and so postpones the same purchases Sam takes, keeping his month open.
- **Payoff:** Paying in pieces changes when the cost is felt, not whether it is paid; flexibility, not money, is what goes missing first.
- **Thumbnail concept, not generated:** Sam at a checkout, one unsplit price tag beside four small cards; text "IT'S ONLY $60". One conflict, one subtraction, no formula.
- **Governing rule:** Every financial concept must read from the animation and the story alone. A viewer who remembers no number should still be able to explain the trap.
- **Production boundary:** Script package only. No TTS, cues, storyboard timing, motion spec, renderer change, render or publishing. Characters follow current project overrides (hairless Sam, blue hoodie; hairless Alex, white shirt and glasses).
