**Fair comparison:** Same offers, same jacket, same instalment terms, same income baseline. Alex differs in one question and therefore in which purchases he completes. He has no extra income, no foresight, no luck, and no rule that Sam could not also use. Neither character is diagnosed; the mechanism is the subject.

**Scope:** The example carries no fees, no interest and no missed payment, so the argument never depends on penalty pricing. It does not claim instalment plans are always harmful, that most users behave like Sam, or that any outcome is guaranteed.

**Deliberately absent:** brand names, market statistics, prevalence claims, credit-score effects, formulas, totals and per-line budgets.
