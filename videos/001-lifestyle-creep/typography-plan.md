# Selective typography plan

V6 is the artwork baseline. This revision changes typography and financial-object motion, not narration, assumptions, audio, scene boundaries or runtime. The legacy screen-text suggestions are superseded by this plan.

Coverage: 67 scenes, 99 panels and 201 source cue beats. 134 beats (66.7%) add no text or new numeric reveal. Counts include ASR fragments and carried visual holds; this is an editorial outcome, not a quota.

Currency already established may remain visible on visual-only beats. FINANCIAL_REVEAL can animate an existing value without adding descriptive text. No subtitle track is created.

Timing is draft ASR. Word ownership, provenance, scene bounds and reading windows are checked before rendering. These checks cannot confirm spoken alignment. No source review flags or cue hashes are rewritten.

All coordinates below belong to the illustrated object space (1920×1080). Callouts share the SVG viewBox; moving an object requires supplying its current coordinates. Headlines emerge from their related object, lists are bound to the existing document or allocation, and counters animate the existing money graphics.

Reading holds are settled frames after the 15-frame entrance and before the 10-frame exit. Numerical counters settle over 18 frames. A cut takes precedence; preparation rejects labels that cannot fit their declared reading hold. Panel offsets of 24 frames stage silent actions within the same cue, not new narration timestamps.
