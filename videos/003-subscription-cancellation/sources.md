# Sources and assumptions

Checked 2026-11-16. US cases and allegations are described within their jurisdiction and historical context.

| Claim | Source and scope |
|---|---|
| Cancellation increased around card replacements; the study modeled inattention and cancellation costs. | [Einav, Klopack and Mahoney, *Selling Subscriptions*, 2025](https://web.stanford.edu/~leinav/pubs/AER2025.pdf). Payment-card data for ten services; model estimates are not universal. |
| News subscription cancellation flows included phone and typed-phrase barriers. | [Sheil et al., *Staying at the Roach Motel*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.17145). Selected news sites in four countries. |
| The FTC alleged difficult Prime cancellation and enrollment without informed consent; the 2025 settlement addressed both and required monetary relief and changes. | [FTC settlement announcement](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/09/ftc-secures-historic-25-billion-settlement-against-amazon); [court order](https://search.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/2024-05-28-ECF165-OrderDenyingDefendants%27MotionstoDismiss.pdf) describes the alleged older four-screen, six-click flow. Not a claim about the current flow. |
| The FTC alleged inadequate disclosure of renewal and cancellation terms and difficult cancellation by Shutterstock; the parties proposed a settlement. | [FTC announcement](https://search.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/05/shutterstock-pay-35-million-settle-ftc-allegations-over-illegal-subscription-cancellation-practices); [joint motion](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/Shutterstock-JointMotion.pdf) states the company neither admitted nor denied the allegations. Case listed as pending at source check. |
| Dark patterns can make subscription cancellation difficult. | [FTC, *Bringing Dark Patterns to Light*, 2022](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/P214800%20Dark%20Patterns%20Report%209.14.2022%20-%20FINAL.pdf?date=2024-04-23). Not every retention offer or confirmation is deceptive. |

The opening offer, buttons, sequence and customer circumstances are invented and do not represent a named provider or actual customer's experience. Account management, contract terms, confirmation and access dates vary by provider. No new narration synthesis, alignment or render is authorized by this script update.
