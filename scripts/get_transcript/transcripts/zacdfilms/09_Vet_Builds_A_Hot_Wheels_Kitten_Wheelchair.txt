TITLE: Vet Builds A Hot Wheels Kitten Wheelchair 😌
URL: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/n-vE95xOVBM

The kitten was badly injured and couldn't walk, but when the vet tried to put him on a wheelchair, he was too small to fit in any of them. So, she started grabbing random items determined to help this kitten walk. She placed cardboard around his body, attached two rulers to one side, and she repeated this on the other side, and strapped it to the kitten along with two Hot Wheels underneath. And when he tried moving, the rulers supported his weight while the tape held everything together, helping him walk for the first time.
