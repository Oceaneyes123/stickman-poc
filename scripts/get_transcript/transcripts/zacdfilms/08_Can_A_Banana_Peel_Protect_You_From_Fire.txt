TITLE: Can A Banana Peel Protect You From Fire? 🤔
URL: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/ZAgoY0dzP2U

If you blow torch a banana and look inside, there's actually water trapped in the peel that begins boiling away before the flame can get to the banana. And even though the outside chars, when you cut it open, the banana inside can still be completely fine. Which means if you somehow made a suit of armor out of a banana peel and then got blasted with a flamethrower, it might provide you with a few seconds of protection before it turns you into banana bread.
