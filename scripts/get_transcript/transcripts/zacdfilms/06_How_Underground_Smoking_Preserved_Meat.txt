TITLE: How Underground Smoking Preserved Meat 🤔
URL: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/6fBvmLxaKdA

The meat is covered in salt, and as it's left to dry, the salt pulls water out, preventing most bacteria from growing. [music] Then, a fire is built inside a small smoke chamber, producing a thick smoke. But, instead of rising straight up, the smoke is pulled into a long tunnel underground. As it travels through the buried passage, it cools down the smoke. Then, it reaches the meat, and the cooled smoke coats the surface, leaving behind chemicals that help prevent the meat [music] from rotting, which allows it to last for weeks.
