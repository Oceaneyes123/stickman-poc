TITLE: How Chaos Theory Works 🤯
URL: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/Jc3PzoA4x14

If you let go of five swinging rods 1° apart, each with another rod attached, they'll start off moving in almost exactly the same way since the rules of physics apply the same to all of them. But that 1° of change at the beginning means each rod is affected slightly differently. And those differences are amplified as [music] they continue to swing. Pretty soon they start moving in ways that appear to be chaotic even though the same laws of physics still apply. And this shows how tiny shifts can have massive effects later down the line. Like the idea of a butterfly flapping its wings in Brazil causing atmospheric changes that eventually cause a tornado in Texas.
