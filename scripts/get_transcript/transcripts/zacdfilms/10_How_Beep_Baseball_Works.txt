TITLE: How Beep Baseball Works 🤔
URL: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/Sk-h6dcaAEY

As the ball flies towards the batter, a tiny speaker inside starts beeping because most of the players on the field can't see. The batter will use the sound to swing his bat at the right time. Then, one of the bases begins to buzz, telling the batter which direction to sprint. Meanwhile, the fielders dive for the beeping ball while yelling to make sure they don't bump [music] into each other. If he grabs the ball before the batter reaches the base, the batter is out. This gives blind people the chance to compete instead of sitting on the sidelines.
