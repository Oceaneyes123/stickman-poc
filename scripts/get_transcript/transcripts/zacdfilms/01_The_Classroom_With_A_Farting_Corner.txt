TITLE: The Classroom With A Farting Corner 🤢
URL: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/16UXIXMq90A

He walked to the corner of the classroom and farted loudly, but nobody reacted. You see, before, whenever someone farted in class, the kids would laugh and blame each other, interrupting the lesson. So their teacher put up a sign in the back corner and told them that whenever they needed to fart, they could walk there without asking permission, and it became known as the gas station.
