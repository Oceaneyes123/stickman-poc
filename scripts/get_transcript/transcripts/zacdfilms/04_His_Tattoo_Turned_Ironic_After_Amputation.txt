TITLE: His Tattoo Turned Ironic After Amputation 😬
URL: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/HoXWgtsx5T8

His tattoo said, "You'll never walk alone." But while fighting in Afghanistan, he stepped on an explosive, which badly damaged his leg. And when doctors later amputated it, they had to remove the section of skin that said alone, meaning the tattoo ended up saying, "You'll never walk." The man actually thought it was funny and didn't let it stop him. After getting a prosthetic leg, he learned to walk again. Then he started running, eventually competing in 6-mi races, and even winning two gold medals, proving his tattoo completely wrong.
