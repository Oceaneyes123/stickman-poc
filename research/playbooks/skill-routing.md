## Choosing a workflow

Use only the skill needed for the current deliverable. A script task does not authorize asset acquisition, TTS generation, component implementation or rendering. Read a selected skill before applying it; distinguish its observed examples from recommendations and verify claims in owning sources. Do not treat a skill's generic formula as a universal YouTube success rule.

The research corpus supplies no private retention, CTR or impression data. Installed skill availability is not proof of missing analytics, licensed media, a working provider integration or an implemented component. Leave unresolved information explicit and preserve the user's authorized phase boundary.
